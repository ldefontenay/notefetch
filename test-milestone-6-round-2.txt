NoteFetch milestone 6, round 2: NVDA, and checking JAWS still works

About 30 minutes: 15 with JAWS, then 15 with NVDA.

What is new:

- NVDA now speaks NoteFetch's announcements. NoteFetch notices when NVDA is
  running and talks to it in the way NVDA listens for. With JAWS it uses the
  same method as before, slightly changed, so section 1 checks that nothing
  has broken.
- The list no longer reads the selected recording again after a sync or a
  refresh, and the window fills its list before it appears.
- Tab in the rename box now finishes the rename, as Enter does, and stays on
  the same recording.
- About NoteFetch is now a small window with the credit, and an OK button.

I tested the NVDA part here myself while you were away, by reading what NVDA
said in its own log, so I expect it to work. I could not test JAWS, which is
why JAWS comes first.

Everything runs on the usual test copy. Please write your answers after each
ANSWER line, and save the file when you are done.
