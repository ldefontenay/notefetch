This is the first test of NoteFetch's own window. It runs on a test copy of
your data, in the NoteFetch folder under test-data, so nothing touches your
Documents folder.

from the window, the question about how much history to download, Settings,
the Help file (F1 does nothing yet), Enter on a recording, the context menu,
renaming and deleting. Those come in milestones 4 to 6.
