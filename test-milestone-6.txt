NoteFetch milestone 6: the guide, announcements and error messages

About 40 minutes, plus 15 for NVDA in section 5 if you have it. Section 6
is reading, at your own pace.

What is new:

- The user guide, opened with F1 from anywhere in NoteFetch, including from
  Settings and the other small windows.
- The Help menu: "NoteFetch guide", "Test a spoken announcement" and
  "About NoteFetch".
- Clearer error messages: every one now says what to do next.

Before you start: a copy of NoteFetch from your last test was still open.
Please close it first with Alt+F4. The new version only starts once that
one is closed.

Everything runs on the usual test copy. Please write your answers after
each ANSWER line, and save the file when you are done.

   Press F1.

   NoteFetch guide in your web browser."

SECTION 4: ERROR MESSAGES

Each message should say what happened and what to do next.

12. Press Alt+L, then N for New folder. Type the name of a folder you
    already have, such as one you made in an earlier test, and press Enter.
    (If you have none, make one called "test" first, then do this step.)
    Expected: "There is already a folder called "...". Choose a different
    name." with the Refused sound.
    Was it so, and is it clear?

ANSWER:

13. Press Alt+C for the list, arrow to a downloaded recording whose status
    is "No transcript" or "Audio only", and press Ctrl+T.
    (Skip this if every recording has a transcript.)
    Expected: it says there is no transcript, and why.
    Was it clear?

ANSWER:

14. Optional, and only if you are happy to: turn off your Wi-Fi or unplug
    the network, then press Ctrl+S in NoteFetch.
    Expected: after a few tries, a message beginning "Could not reach
    Plaud", ending "Check your internet connection, then try again.", with
    the Refused sound. Turn the network back on, and press Ctrl+S again:
    the sync works.
