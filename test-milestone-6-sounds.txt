NoteFetch milestone 6 test: the sound palette, first draft

About 20 minutes. NoteFetch now has four little sounds of its own, made the
same way as TweakSound's: the same lead-in of a twelfth of a second you
chose there, and its volume as "Normal". The notes are new, and none of
them is a note StableSound or TweakSound plays.

- Done: two quick notes rising. A change you made worked: a rename, move,
  delete, skip or folder.
- Finished: Done's two notes and then a higher one, like Done's bigger
  sibling. Something you started has finished: a sync, a download, a
  sign-in, or moving your recordings to a new output folder.
- Refused: two notes falling, to an unsettled-sounding note. Something
  could not be done, or went wrong.
- Attention: one note, a short gap, then the same note higher, like a
  call. NoteFetch has a question for you.

They play only for what you did. The sync when NoteFetch starts is silent,
unless something goes wrong.

Nothing is fixed yet: pitches, lengths, volume and which events get a sound
can all change. Describe anything in your own words.

Please write your answers after each ANSWER line. Leave any you cannot do
blank, and say so. Save the file when you are done.
